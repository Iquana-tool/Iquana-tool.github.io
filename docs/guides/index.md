# Guides

Task-by-task walkthroughs. Each one assumes you have IQUANA
[installed and running](../getting-started/install.md).

If you are starting from nothing, follow them roughly in this order.

<div class="iq-cards" markdown>

[**Create a dataset**<span>Upload images and give them typed metadata.</span>](create-a-dataset.md)

[**Build a label hierarchy**<span>Define what you are annotating, and how it nests.</span>](build-a-label-hierarchy.md)

[**Annotate with AI**<span>Point, box, polygon and freehand prompting.</span>](annotate-with-ai.md)

[**Draw and refine manually**<span>When the model is not the right tool.</span>](draw-and-refine.md)

[**Calibrate to physical units**<span>Turn pixels into millimetres.</span>](calibrate.md)

[**Run batch inference**<span>Segment a whole dataset in the background.</span>](batch-inference.md)

[**Review and correct**<span>Route predictions to reviewers and fix what is wrong.</span>](review-and-correct.md)

[**Quantify and export**<span>Compute metrics and get them out.</span>](quantify-and-export.md)

[**Train a model**<span>Fine-tune on your corrected annotations.</span>](train-a-model.md)

[**Manage access**<span>Invite people and give them the right role.</span>](manage-access.md)

</div>
